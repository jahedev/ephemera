## Detailed description

Every time you open a tab, Ephemera paints one. Nine generative styles, eighteen
colour palettes, randomized parameters — pulled once, then gone. If one is worth
keeping, press the star and it stays; press download and it re-renders at 4K,
pixel for pixel the image you were looking at.

Everything on top of the print is optional and configurable:

**Clock** — 12 or 24 hour, with or without seconds and the date.

**Search that knows more than one destination** — Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing,
YouTube, Gmail, Google Maps, Amazon, Wayback Machine, GitHub, Wikipedia, Reddit,
ChatGPT and Claude are built in, and you can add your own. Choose which appear
and in what order, or type a bang — `!gh rust`, `!yt`, `!wb` — to jump to one for
a single search.

**Pinned sites** — tiles with real favicons or generated monograms, reorderable
by dragging, openable with the number keys.

**Sticky notes** — drag them anywhere, resize them, six colours, saved as you
type.

**Nothing leaves your machine.** Ephemera makes no network requests at all. There
is no analytics, no account, no telemetry, and no remote code. Prints are
generated on your computer from a single random number. Your settings and pins
sync through your own Chrome profile; your notes never leave the device. There is
an export button so you can take a copy with you.

## Single purpose

Ephemera replaces the new tab page. Everything it does — the generated wallpaper,
the clock, the search bar, the pinned sites and the notes — exists to make that
one page useful, and each part can be switched off.

## Permission justifications

**storage** — Saves your settings, pinned sites, kept prints and notes so they
survive between tabs. Settings and pins use `storage.sync` so they follow your
Chrome profile; notes stay in `storage.local` on this device. No data is sent
anywhere.

**favicon** — Reads icons the browser has *already* cached for sites you have
pinned, so a pin tile shows the site's own icon instead of a placeholder. This
reads the local favicon store and makes no network request. Without it, pins fall
back to generated monogram tiles.

**Host permissions** — none requested.

**Remote code** — none. All scripts ship inside the package.

## Notes on review

Extensions that override the new tab page get extra scrutiny, and Chrome will
show users a "keep this change?" prompt on install. That is expected. The
override is the item's entire purpose and is declared in
`chrome_url_overrides.newtab`.
